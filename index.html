Pollution

Ocean Pollution is a major threat to the environment and a leading cause of our sea life being endangered.This website was developed to spread knowledge about this catastrophe and give people a way to make a difference.



Welcome to PollutionSolution, your starting point for clean oceans. Our goal is to show how waste harms marine life and how you can help fix it. Explore our pages to learn what causes this mess, how it hurts nature, and what steps you can take today. Ocean pollution brings millions of tons of plastic trash, chemical runoff, and toxic oil into our seas every year, choking sea turtles, poisoning fish, and destroying vital coral reefs.
